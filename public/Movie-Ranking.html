The Best Movies According To Shiva

My Top 3 Movies of all-time.

The Social Network

A movie that boggles the mind and inspires you to achieve something bigger in your life.

Avengers:End Game

This film is the pure definition of an emotional roller coaster and throughout its run time brings about fascination, humor, sadness, incredible excitement, and sheer finality.

Inside Out

The story is both touching and thought-provoking, addressing themes of identity, change, and the bittersweet nature of growing up.
